# Motion reference study

- [BUCK — Microsoft app](https://buck.co/work/microsoft-app): studied keyframes
  from the official directors' cut. The useful principle is a clear relationship
  between product detail and the complete product. Apply it through camera
  distance, depth and readable holds. The previous rope and cropped screenshot
  panels did not communicate that relationship and have been removed.
- FireFit / “Project15 App UI Promo Adobe AE”, the existing local reference:
  studied the 23-second reel's front-facing hardware turns and alternating wide
  and macro views. Use that camera rhythm while keeping one continuous device.
- [Raycast, on the go](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXrq58zBdl8) and
  [Linear Mobile](https://linear.app/mobile): studied for short product claims
  and authentic UI evidence. Their live-action visual direction was not used
  for the requested 3D treatment.

The reference videos remain research material under the ignored references
folder. No reference footage or soundtrack is included in Whip's composition.
