Connect to your own machines

Your first connection.

You need a reachable SSH host. Install Herdr on that host for integrated agent and workspace features.

  1. Install Whip

    Get the app for Android 7.0 or newer, or iPhone and iPad running iOS / iPadOS 16.4 or newer.

  2. Add your host

    Enter your SSH details and verify the host fingerprint. Or run uvx whipair on the host, scan its QR code in Whip, and approve your public key on the host.

  3. Open your workspace

    Connect to your Herdr session and follow your agents. Ordinary SSH terminals and SFTP also work without Herdr.

Agent software and model-provider accounts are configured on your host. No additional Whip server is required when SSH and Herdr are already running. Opt-in remote push notifications use a separate host plugin.

A few things to know

About Whip.

Is Whip the same app as Whip Herd?

Yes. Whip is listed as Whip Herd on the App Store and Whip on Google Play. Earlier iOS names include Whip SSH and Whip Herdr. The source project is kosumic/whip on GitHub, maintained by Kosumi (KANGCHENG CHEN).

Can I use Claude Code or Codex from my phone?

Yes. Run Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, or Pi inside Herdr on your remote machine, then connect with Whip to follow conversations and send instructions. The agents run on your host, not on your phone. Whip is an independent app and is not endorsed by Herdr or the supported agent providers.

Do I need Herdr?

Herdr is required for integrated agent supervision and workspace features. You can use Whip as a regular SSH and SFTP client without it. First-class multiplexer integration is built around Herdr.

What is whipair?

whipair is Whip’s optional command-line SSH public-key pairing helper. Run uvx whipair or npx whipair on your host and scan the QR code in the mobile app. You can also enter SSH details manually. Read the pairing guide.

Is Whip free and open source?

Core SSH, SFTP, terminal, and Herdr features are free. The optional Rancher in-app purchase adds appearance options. Whip’s source is available under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Rights in the Whip name and logo and the Rancher and Cowboy illustrations are reserved. See the branding and artwork notice.

How does Whip connect to my computer?

Whip connects to your host over SSH, directly or through saved jump hosts. Tailcat mode is also available for connecting across networks. Credentials are protected by Android Keystore or iOS Keychain. See the security policy and privacy notes for details, including optional services.
